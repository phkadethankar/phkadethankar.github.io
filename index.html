About

Piyush H. Kadethankar is a Research Assistant at Alliance Manchester Business School, The University of Manchester, where he works with Professor Peter J. Buckley. His research interests lie in international business, particularly the strategies of multinational enterprises (MNEs) and middle-power economies under geopolitical rivalry. His parallel strand of research focuses on the role of artificial intelligence in managerial and entrepreneurial decision-making. His research has earned multiple R&Rs from prestigious journals such as Journal of International Business Studies and Small Business Economics.

He completed his MSc in International Business and Management at Alliance Manchester Business School in September 2026, with a dissertation supervised by Professor Mark P. Healey. Before that, he earned a B.Tech. in Electronics and Telecommunication Engineering from SGGS Institute of Engineering and Technology, Nanded (affiliated with Swami Ramanand Teerth Marathwada University), where he worked with Professor Ramchandra R. Manthalkar on EEG signal processing and applied machine learning.
